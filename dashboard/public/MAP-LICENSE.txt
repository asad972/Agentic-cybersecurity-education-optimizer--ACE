This local derivative keeps country code, English name and continent; rounds
coordinates to 4 decimal places and omits Antarctica. ISO_A2_EH is used when
available; Natural Earth ADM0_A3 is retained otherwise for display only.
Boundaries reflect Natural Earth's cartographic conventions and are not a
statement about sovereignty. Small countries may require zooming or the
accessible country selector.
