# M1 taxonomy contribution sources

Retrieved and checked on **2026-10-27**.

## Scope
This contribution covers core Security Operations Center (SOC), Threat Analysis, Network Traffic Analysis, and Penetration Testing skills and certifications owned by M1. Canonical names match the test datasets and profile fixtures.

## ECSF role mappings
Source: [ENISA European Cybersecurity Skills Framework Role Profiles](https://www.enisa.europa.eu/sites/default/files/publications/European%20Cybersecurity%20Skills%20Framework%20Role%20Profiles.pdf)
- **Cyber Incident Responder**: Identifies, manages, investigates, and responds to cybersecurity incidents. Applies to SIEM, Splunk, Incident response, and Wireshark.
- **Cybersecurity Operations**: Operates and monitors operational security controls.
- **Cyber Threat Intelligence Specialist**: Assesses potential threats and vulnerability vectors. Applies to penetration testing, Metasploit, Burp Suite, and Nmap.
